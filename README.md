>> ## *What is VisionQuest?*
>>> VisionQuest *(or Vision Quest)* can refer to a Marvel Cinematic Universe television miniseries or a traditional Indigenous rite of passage. It is a spin off series after the events of *WandaVision*, where a rebooted White Vision *(played by Paul Bettany)* lives in hiding in Scotland while trying to piece together his identity and memories. Some of the characters are inside a virtual "mindspace" *(a manor in his own head)*, he consults legacy Stark AIs—including J.A.R.V.I.S., F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., and an imprisoned Ultron *(James Spader)*. His isolation ends when a bounty forces him on the run alongside Thomas Shepherd *(Ruaridh Mollica)*, a teenager who may be his reincarnated son.

>>> Well for me, I think the show is a solid 8.7/10, a lot of the scenes were great and I love the characteristic and personalities of each character, especially the Stark-AI's and the humorous-yet-evil personality of Ultron.
